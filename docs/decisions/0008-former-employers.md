# 0008 Never name a former employer critically

Status: Accepted
Date: 2026-11-23

## Decision

No page, draft or doc in this repo names a former employer critically or criticises its people or tools. Which roles appear on the page is Hugh's call; the fleet never adds one.

## Why

Employers read this page (docs/VISION.md "Why it exists"), and Hugh's rules forbid criticising a former employer, its people or tools in anything external (Career-Business STATE, Project rules).
